How to Play 16 Goti

Master the 37-point Sholo Guti strategy board

  • 16 Soldiers Each: Red and Blue each command 16 beads (Goti) across the 5×5 Alquerque grid and two triangular camps.
  • Movement: Tap one of your beads, then tap any highlighted adjacent empty point connected by a line.
  • Capturing: Jump over an adjacent opponent bead along a straight line onto the empty point directly behind it (+1 Score).
  • Multi-Jump Chains: If a capture jump lands where another jump is open, chain multiple jumps in one turn!
  • Victory: Capture all 16 opponent beads or trap the opponent with no legal moves.
